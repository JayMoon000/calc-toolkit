Text Metrics Explained

1. Characters vs. Bytes

A character is a human-readable symbol, whereas a byte represents physical storage memory. For standard English, 1 character = 1 byte. For international scripts (Korean, Chinese, Arabic) and modern emoji, a single glyph requires 3 to 4 bytes in UTF-8 databases.

2. Speech vs. Reading Speed

Silent reading occurs at roughly 200–250 words per minute (WPM), while natural speaking pace for presentations, podcasts, and speeches averages 130–150 WPM.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is there any character limit for analysis on this page?

No. Since execution runs locally in your browser without hitting an API endpoint, you can paste entire book chapters or research papers with no server quotas or size caps.

Q. Does deleting my text also delete it from your database?

There is no database. Nothing is ever sent to or saved on an external server. Once you clear the box or close the tab, your text is completely erased from your browser's temporary memory.

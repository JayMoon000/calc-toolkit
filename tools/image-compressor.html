Web Performance Optimization Guidelines

1. The 80% Quality Sweet Spot

Reducing image quality from 100% to 80% typically strips 50% to 70% of the byte weight while retaining structural sharpness that is imperceptible to the human eye.

2. Local Browser Compute Architecture

By executing compression via browser-native Canvas APIs, high-volume image batches are processed without waiting in cloud queues or uploading sensitive internal screenshots to third-party servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Does this tool store or log image metadata (EXIF)?

No. During canvas drawing, EXIF metadata (GPS coordinates, camera serial numbers) is automatically stripped, protecting your privacy before web publishing.

Q. Are there limits on the number of photos I can compress?

Zero limits. The tool executes directly on your hardware's CPU and memory, entirely free of external API quotas.

QR Code Deployment Best Practices

1. Static Codes vs. Dynamic Shortlinks

Static QR codes encode actual strings into the matrix. They never expire, require no subscription renewals, and cannot be intercepted by redirect services.

2. Contrast & Quiet Zone Rules

Maintain a minimum 4-module quiet zone (white border) around the outer edges of the code matrix. Optical scanner cameras require this margin to detect positioning landmarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can Wi-Fi QR codes connect iOS and Android devices without typing?

Yes. Native camera applications on modern iOS and Android parse WIFI:S:... strings directly, prompting an instant "Join Network" dialog with zero manual password entry.

Q. Are my contact details or URLs saved to a database?

Never. All calculations run strictly in your browser runtime memory. No data is transmitted across an external network.

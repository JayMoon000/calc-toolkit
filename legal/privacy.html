Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Starsign16 Toolkit is built on a 100% client-side architecture. When you enter financial numbers, loan figures, personal text, passwords, or image files into any of our calculators or utilities, none of that information is ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Browser LocalStorage

Certain offline tools, such as the 2048 puzzle game, may record non-personally identifiable state data (such as your personal best high score) directly in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`). This data resides strictly on your local device and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Cookies

We may utilize privacy-friendly standard web analytics or Google services to monitor high-level aggregated site traffic, browser compatibility, and performance metrics. These tools do not record sensitive user inputs from our calculation engines.

4. Policy Updates

Any revisions to this privacy policy will be posted immediately on this page with an updated timestamp.
